Why “jumping gene”?

Transposable elements are often nicknamed “jumping genes” because they can move or copy themselves to new locations in a genome.

How to read these layers

Structure, domains and primer geometry answer different questions. Examine them together, and keep a missing or failed analysis separate from a negative result. Designed primers still need laboratory validation.
