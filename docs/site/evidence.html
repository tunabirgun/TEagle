What changed in 4.1.1?

Version 4.1.1 corrects the displayed included-region interval: a stored start and length are shown as the corresponding start and end. Scientific calculations and parameters are unchanged.

The decision to use targeted verification instead of a full scientific benchmark repeat is documented as Deviation 7. The scientific evaluation remains identified as 4.1.0.

Computational evidence and experimental validation

The exploratory biological series connects assessment, design and predicted products across ten records in seven dependence blocks. It includes unsuccessful design outcomes and separate generic routes; it is not a wet-lab validation study.

Use predicted products to plan experiments, not as a substitute for confirming product identity, specificity or quantitative performance.
