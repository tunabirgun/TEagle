Primer design & predicted products

Turn a region into an assay proposal.

Choose the biological question first. Then inspect where the oligos bind and what the proposed pair could amplify.

Choose the design region

  • Within a protein domain: use detected domain evidence to define a region of interest.
  • Across an insertion junction: plan around the boundary and available flanking sequence.
  • Custom range: specify the part of the sequence appropriate to your question.

TEagle uses Primer3 to propose pairs and shows their positions and secondary-structure checks. A custom range does not by itself make an assay TE-specific.

Inspect the pair and its products

Review the forward and reverse oligos, the selected constraints and the predicted products. Use the to-scale gel to inspect product sizes and the tables for exact values.

Multiple predicted products are a result to investigate, not a reason to report only the intended band. If no acceptable pair is returned, review the sequence and constraints rather than treating an empty table as a successful design.

The website’s map is illustrative. The desktop application performs the analysis. Primer design and in-silico products do not establish amplification efficiency, laboratory specificity or qPCR performance.

Check the intended background

Candidate priming-site searches can use a selected downloaded assembly and fixed criteria. Record which assembly and criteria were used. A reference-background check is different from testing your actual biological material.

Export a usable assay record

Keep the oligo order sheet, predicted-product table and assay report together. The order sheet supports XLSX, CSV and TSV; the assay report supports PDF and HTML.
