Interpretation

Read the evidence before the label.

TEagle connects sequence features with classification, while keeping unsupported interpretations visible.

Structure and protein domains

Inspect terminal features, open reading frames and domain matches in the feature map. Paired terminal direct repeats alone do not establish an LTR order. A domain supports an interpretation within its model and search scope.

The bundled 30-model Pfam panel provides TE-domain evidence. Isolated domains do not force a finer lineage call. Review the method and the matched region rather than treating every displayed feature as equally decisive.

Classification and family annotation

Evidence-linked Wicker class, order and superfamily calls appear with a rank path and a RepeatMasker-style class string. Unsupported ranks are withheld.

Optional Dfam matching adds family annotations. Coverage depends on the installed library: a family absent from that library cannot be reported. Transcript-guided splice detection adds a separate type of evidence.

Read the run state

StateWhat to retain when interpreting it
CompleteThe stage finished within its stated scope; this is not a guarantee of biological truth.
PartialSome evidence is available. Read what could not be completed.
WithheldThe software does not make the unsupported call.
FailedThe stage encountered an error; do not interpret it as no matches.
Not runThe stage was not executed.

Keep the scale of the question clear

The central workflow evaluates an individual sequence or locus. Optional whole-genome RepeatMasker landscape annotation is a separate function; assembly-wide results do not turn a single-locus assessment into de-novo TE-family discovery.

Assembly candidate-site checks are limited to the selected assembly and search criteria. They do not establish specimen-wide specificity.
